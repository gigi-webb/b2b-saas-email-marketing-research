## Key Research Findings

### Newsletter Acquisition Cost Economics

**Primary Thesis:**
Paid acquisition for newsletters ($0.50-$2.00 per subscriber) is viable but requires different financial models than organic growth.

**Monetization Economics:**
- Organic-growth newsletters: ~$0.05-$0.15 CAC, 12-18 month payback period
- Paid-acquisition newsletters: ~$0.50-$2.00 CAC, requires clear monetization path within 3-6 months
- Subscription tiers (freemium + paid): Best ROI for paid acquisition cohorts

## Application to Playbook: Win-Back Sequences

### Critical Insight on Win-Back Economics

**McGarry's Framework:**
Win-back sequences only work if initial subscriber acquisition cost (CAC) is factored into lifetime value (LTV) calculations.

**The Math:**
If acquisition_cost > $1/subscriber, then win_back_ROI_only_positive_if LTV >= $12 (12-month breakeven) OR segment_is_high_value (strategic accounts).

**Practical Implication for Playbook:**
- High-LTV segments: Run full win-back sequence (3 emails, aggressive re-engagement)
- Low-LTV segments: Single win-back email only, then remove
